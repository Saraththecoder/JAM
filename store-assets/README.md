## Still required before submission

These cannot be generated without the deployed app and real accounts:

- At least 2 phone screenshots (up to 8). Use 1080 × 1920 or 1440 × 2560 PNG/JPEG.
- Optional 7-inch and 10-inch tablet screenshots if you want tablet featuring.
- A short description (80 characters max).
- A full description.
- App access instructions and a working test account for Play review.
- A public privacy policy URL: `https://YOUR_DOMAIN/privacy`.
- Data safety answers.
- Content rating questionnaire.
- Account deletion flow/link (see `PLAY_STORE.md`).

Do not use fake screenshots or screenshots from another app. Play review compares
the listing with the installed app.

## Suggested screenshots

1. Student dashboard with letter statuses.
2. New letter form / AI draft step.
3. Mentor review screen with signature and approval actions.
4. HOD approval screen.
5. QR verification page showing an approved letter.

Use a test account with realistic but non-sensitive sample data. Do not expose a
real student's roll number, signature, or letter body in store screenshots.
